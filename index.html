About Nasibe

NASIBE is a tribute to the enduring power of memory. Founded by Dafne Haktan Karkalem, the house is named for her late mother, Nasibe Çiğseren, a woman whose quiet elegance and strength remain the brand's eternal muse.

Born from an intimate dialogue between mother and daughter, the collection imagines the pieces she would have worn, loved, and cherished: objects that bridge the gap between the past and the present.

At the heart of the brand is a reverence for living materials. By sculpting in wood—warm, organic, and distinct—NASIBE celebrates the beauty of imperfection and the soul of the hand-made.

Story of Nasibe

Founded by Dafne Haktan Karkalem, NASIBE is a deeply personal tribute to her late mother. Viewing design as a medium for emotional expression, Dafne channeled her grief into creativity, turning abstract memories into sculptural forms.

The Makers

NASIBE is proud to preserve the vanishing art of the Grand Bazaar. Every piece in our collection is entirely handmade in Istanbul, bridging the gap between ancient craftsmanship and contemporary design. Each link is individually hand-carved, sanded, and polished — every NASIBE creation is a "Piece Unique."

About Dafne

Our Founder

Born in Istanbul and shaped by a life divided between Istanbul and London, Dafne Haktan Karkalem brings a distinct duality to the world of fine jewelry. Her aesthetic navigates the space between the historic and the contemporary, drawing on the rich, artisanal heritage of Istanbul.

For Dafne, NASIBE was born from a desire to give physical form to memory. As Founder and Creative Director, Dafne approaches jewelry not merely as ornamentation, but as emotional architecture.
